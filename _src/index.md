Hi there! I'm Rand, a junior researcher and teaching assistant at
[Grenoble INP](https://www.grenoble-inp.fr/en), France.
I'm specialized in control theory, optimization, and mathematical modeling. 

My main research topic is optimization and control of synthetic microbial
consortia in continuous bioreactors.
This includes biological modeling, observer design, and feedback control
for nonlinear systems.

## Research interests

- Kalman filtering and nonlinear observers
- Nonlinear optimization and optimal control strategies
- Receding horizon control and moving horizon estimation
